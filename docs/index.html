Why it exists

A router for chemistry judgment, not a black box.

Right method for the system

Organic, metallic, protein-like, and large ML-potential cases take different paths, even when the user asks the same plain-language question.

Limits stated up front

xTB size cliffs, Gaussian license gates, Amber scope, and ML potential uncertainty are surfaced as part of the answer.

Validation before confidence

MACE MD is cross-validated against GFN2-xTB every 1 ps by default and aborts when the force error crosses the documented threshold.

Sibling skills

Two focused surfaces, one design contract.

ASE, xTB, MACE, Gaussian

ase-chemist

Atomistic and molecular simulation through ASE with seven backends, explicit Gaussian method inputs, and ML potential validation.

  • Geometry optimization and molecular dynamics
  • GFN2-xTB, MACE-MP-0, MACE-OFF, EMT, LJ, TIP3P
  • Gaussian single-point, optimization, frequency, and thermochemistry
Read ase-chemist docs

AmberTools, pmemd, cpptraj

amber-chemist

Amber-native MD for workflows where restart handling, T-REMD, implicit solvent, cpptraj analysis, and MMPBSA matter.

  • Single-replica MD with restart and extension
  • Temperature replica-exchange MD with demux support
  • Endpoint scoring, alanine scans, and trajectory analysis

Backends

The route depends on chemistry, scale, and installed tools.

Backend Reach for it when Skill
GFN2-xTB Organic and main-group systems up to the practical xTB size cliff. ase-chemist
MACE Larger organic or materials MD, guarded by mandatory xTB cross-validation. ase-chemist
Gaussian DFT Publication-grade SP, optimization, frequency, and thermochemistry. ase-chemist
Amber Production MD, T-REMD, implicit solvent, cpptraj analysis, and MMPBSA. amber-chemist
